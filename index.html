the problem

we only review when exams force us to.

Concepts click in class, then sit in 60-slide decks nobody reopens. Reviewing feels like a whole separate study session, so it waits. Then exam week hits and we relearn the semester from generic videos that don’t match what our professor taught.

how loop. works

five minutes. your course. on repeat.

1

swipe to learn

Short visual explanations built from your own lecture slides and readings, 30 to 90 seconds each.

2

swipe to test

Every few cards, one quick question so you actively recall instead of just watching.

3

swipe to reinforce

What you miss comes back before your exam. Every card links to its source slide.

why not just…

built for revision, not for prompting.

What you use nowWhat loop. adds
ChatGPTNo prompts to write, no re-explaining your course every time.
NotebookLMA continuous, bite-sized feed instead of a sit-down document session.
TikTok & YouTubeContent matched to your course, your professor and your syllabus.
Quizlet & AnkiVisual explanations together with active recall and smart review.
Lecture slidesQuick reinforcement without opening a 60-slide deck.

loop. doesn’t replace studying. It fills the gap between the lecture and sitting down to study.
